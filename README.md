# tpm-lab

Working models for programs I am thinking through now, built from what I know from the ones I have run.

Everything here is in progress. Each folder starts with research, then a charter, then the plan for running it. Expect gaps, open questions and changes as the research lands. When a model is solid enough to use, it stays here; the status line on each folder says how far along it is.

---

## What Is Here

| Folder | Question it answers | Status |
|:---|:---|:---|
| [Post-Quantum Cryptography](post-quantum-cryptography/) | How do you upgrade cryptography across a very large enterprise for the post-quantum transition? | 🚧 In progress: research checked; charter and 90-day plan in first draft |
| [Optimizing a PMO](optimizing-a-pmo/) | You take over a PMO, and the business wants more value from it. The cause is usually the setup, not the people in it. What do you do first, and over the first year? | 🚧 In progress: first principles, stakeholders, the three levels, NPV, charters, playbook, operating model, evidence |

---

## How Each Model Is Built

1. **Research** from primary sources: the standard, the official guidance, the paper. Sources are linked and dated.
2. **Charter**: the problem, the outcomes, the owners, and what is out of scope.
3. **The plan**: phases, decision points, and what usually goes wrong at each one.

Examples are illustrative. Nothing here describes a specific employer's systems or figures.
