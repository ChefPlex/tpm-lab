# PMO Turnaround

**Status:** 🚧 In progress: research

You walk in the door of a PMO the company does not value or like. That view usually reaches past the office itself, to the program, portfolio and project managers in it. How do you turn it around so the company sees the function as worth having?

What do you do first, second and third? What do you do in the first 30, 60, 90, 180 and 365 days?

## What Will Be Here

- **Research notes**: how other large companies have organized and run program and project management, and what the evidence says about why PMOs lose standing.
- **Diagnostic**: the first job is finding out why the PMO is not valued, before changing anything.
- **Charter**: what the turnaround commits to, and how anyone will know it worked.
- **The plan**: 30, 60, 90, 180 and 365 days.
