# Measuring a PMO

**Status:** 🚧 In progress

The measures for optimizing a PMO, in one place. Most of them already appear in the other pages; this page gathers them, says how to measure each one, how often, and whether it carries a target.

## Three rules first

- **Targets on the PMO's own measures; trends on teams' delivery.** The PMO can be held to targets, money included. Team delivery measures are shown as trends against each team's own history, never as targets or rankings, because a target turns a measure into something to steer ([operating model](operating-model.md#dashboards-and-comparisons), [evidence](evidence.md#metrics)).
- **Someone outside the PMO checks each definition.** Finance for the money, engineering leads for reporting hours, the partner groups for trust. A PMO that defines and grades its own measures is not measuring much.
- **Check the data before it counts.** Sample the tracker data and publish its error rate before any of these reach leadership.

Measures marked **(leading, untested)** are early signals that seem useful in practice. No published study has validated a set of leading indicators for programs, so treat them as hypotheses and watch whether they actually predict anything.

## 1. Value: the money ledger

Agreed with Finance, reviewed every quarter. See [the money ledger](operating-model.md#the-money-ledger).

| Measure | How to measure | Target or trend |
|:---|:---|:---|
| PMO cost | Fully loaded annual cost of the office | Target |
| Capacity freed | Work stopped or merged by portfolio decisions, at loaded cost | Target |
| Spend avoided | Commitments not made, vendors not engaged, duplicate work stopped | Target |
| Cost of delay | On the five largest programs, estimated as Finance and Product agree | Trend |
| Return on the PMO | (Capacity freed + spend avoided) ÷ PMO cost | Target |
| Finance and audit data intact | Capitalization and audit data still flowing (yes or no) | Must stay yes |

## 2. Portfolio health

| Measure | How to measure | Target or trend |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Charter coverage | Share of tier 1 and 2 work with a current, signed charter | Target: all of it |
| NPV coverage | Share of discretionary tier 1 and 2 work with an NPV under a quarter old | Target |
| Inventory scored | Share of the inventory of work scored with the same model | Target: all of it |
| Stop decisions | Items stopped or merged each quarter, and the capacity it freed | Trend |
| Capacity by layer | Share of capacity in run-the-business, regulatory, committed, discretionary and buffer | Trend, agreed with the sponsor |
| Benefits realized | Benefits confirmed by their owners, against the charter | Trend |
| Colour pattern | Share of programs that are mostly green, sometimes yellow; flag those always green or always yellow ([first principles](first-principles.md#reading-red-amber-and-green)) | Trend |

## 3. Predictability

All trends, against each team's own history.

| Measure | How to measure |
|:---|:---|
| Forecast reliability | Share of milestones that land inside their forecast range |
| Yellow before red | Share of red items that were yellow in an earlier report |
| Latitude used | Share of the 10 to 20 percent time margin each program has used ([re-baseline](first-principles.md#re-baseline-once-with-a-learning-margin)) |
| Re-baselines | How many times each program has re-baselined; the aim is once |
| Benefit dates | Benefits arriving within their forecast range |

## 4. Flow (leading, untested)

| Measure | How to measure | Target or trend |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Blocker clearance | Working days from a blocker reaching the PMO to it being cleared | Target, on the PMO |
| Dependency age | Open dependencies past their committed date, and by how long | Trend |
| Decision age | Decisions past the date they were due | Trend |
| Load per manager | Programs or projects each manager carries | Agreed limit |

## 5. Stakeholders

See [Stakeholders](stakeholders.md).

| Measure | How to measure | Target or trend |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Trust | Quarterly short survey per partner group, on ability, benevolence and integrity | Target: up in every group |
| Their own measure | Each group's own success measure, from the stakeholder register | Trend |
| Requests on their own initiative | How often teams ask for PMO help without it being required | Trend |
| Sponsor engagement | Sponsor attendance at the portfolio review, and the standing slot held | Target |
| Follow-through | Share of items raised by stakeholders or PMO staff that reached a result the person was told about ([Heard, and Followed Through](heard-and-followed-through.md)) | Target |
| Feeling heard | "When you raise something with the PMO, do you see it lead somewhere?", in the quarterly check | Target: up |

## 6. How the PMO runs

| Measure | How to measure | Target or trend |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Reporting load | Hours a week teams spend producing PMO reporting, measured by asking them | Target: lower |
| Mechanisms | Recurring meetings and reports, added and retired each quarter; each has an owner and an expiry date | Target: one retired for each added |
| Reports that drive decisions | Share of reports with a named person who decides from them | Target |
| Data quality | Error rate in sampled tracker data | Target: published, then falling |

## 7. People in the PMO

| Measure | How to measure | Target or trend |
|:---|:---|:---|
| The agreement, both ways | "Does the PMO give you what you need?" and "Do we get what we need to help?" ([the agreement](three-levels.md#the-agreement-between-the-pmo-and-its-managers)) | Trend |
| Retention | Managers who choose to stay, against the company rate | Trend |
| Growth | Development plans in place; moves into larger roles | Target |
| Workload | Managers within the agreed load limit | Target |

## The sponsor's one page

Eight measures, one per line, every quarter. The rest stays with the PMO and the teams.

| | Measure |
|:---|:---|
| 1 | Return on the PMO: capacity freed plus spend avoided, against its cost |
| 2 | Charter and NPV coverage of tier 1 and 2 work |
| 3 | Forecast reliability, as a trend |
| 4 | Days to clear a blocker |
| 5 | Hours teams spend on PMO reporting |
| 6 | Trust, by partner group |
| 7 | The colour pattern across the portfolio |
| 8 | Retention and growth of the PMO's own people |

## Getting started

- **Take the baseline in weeks 3 to 6,** during the [diagnosis](playbook.md#weeks-3-to-6-diagnose), before anything changes. Without it, no later number means anything.
- **Write each definition down,** with the person who checks it, and publish the list.
- **Start with the sponsor's eight.** Add the rest as the data becomes reliable enough to use.
- **Retire a measure no decision depends on,** the same test the PMO applies to its reports.
